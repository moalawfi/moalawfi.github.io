01 About

I'm an Artificial Intelligence student at Taibah University in Medina and a researcher in Natural Language Processing, GeoAI and Computer Vision.

Most of my work starts from a gap I care about: Arabic, and especially Saudi dialects, is underserved by mainstream NLP. I co-authored a human-validated Saudi Arabic intent benchmark and presented it at IEEE MCAIT 2026. I also like implementing algorithms from scratch, such as gradient descent and PCA, before reaching for libraries.

02 Research

IEEE MCAIT 2026 🎤 Presenter 📚 To appear in IEEE Xplore

A Human-Validated Saudi Arabic Intent Benchmark Dataset with Leakage-Aware Splits and Evaluation Across Dialects and Model Families

M. A. Alawfi, A. M. Almutairi, Y. M. Althubyani, I. F. Moawad

10,500utterances
30intent labels
4language varieties

Covers Hijazi, Najdi, Shared Saudi Gulf and MSA, validated by three domain experts, with leakage-aware splits. Evaluates classical classifiers, fine-tuned transformers, embedding models and prompted LLMs.
